Tune: set up on the same picture you play
  1. The TUNE band is blue, never red or green, so it can't be mistaken for LIVE. Show lock turns Tune off.
  2. Click a pad and it turns over in place. HIT's back holds its pool, how a press behaves, and what else it moves.
  3. The mini cue picker marks what each role already uses. Audience cues are hatched with the reason (V6).
  4. Reactions are added from template chips. Each one shows its curve and length in beats.
  5. The other pads show a short summary and "edit". BLACKOUT's back says it is fixed.
  6. Every change is a normal undoable edit. The Vibes tab stays for bulk work across vibes.
