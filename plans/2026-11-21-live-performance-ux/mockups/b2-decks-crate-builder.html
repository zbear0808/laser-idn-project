B2 · build a crate in under a minute
  1. Library: the 8 × 4 grid as thumbnails. Search and tag chips narrow it; marks show which crates use a cue.
  2. Multi-select, then drag onto a crate (or ⏎). A smart crate fills itself from tag rules.
  3. Crate cards say if they are ready, with friendly hints ("HIT has 1 cue — it will repeat"). Hints never block.
  4. Roles auto-fill (AUTO bar, one Undo). Each cue has a drag handle to move it to another role.
  5. Live drag: beam fan leaves ACCENT for the empty BED; the hint says what that will change.
  6. Audition plays the crate on the NEXT preview only. The lasers don't change.
