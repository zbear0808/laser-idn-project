B3 · when a vibe leaves, where it goes, how it changes
  1. While the show is live, mini decks stay pinned on the vibe tabs: NOW, NEXT and the take.
  2. Auto-advance on the deck: an AUTO tag, a countdown ring ("5 bars") and lilac bars at the end of NOW's bar counter. Hold VIBE to stay.
  3. Set path: one block per vibe, as wide as its bars; arrows are follow actions (next, energy up, random, stay). The white line is the playhead.
  4. Blind edit of the cued vibe: amber hatched frame, CHANGED marks, a list of what is not on air yet, and one Commit (r04 rule 19).
  5. Preview of the take plays the chosen transition on the NEXT preview only. Discard or Commit; the pads keep working throughout.
