D2 · sorting replaces filling slots
  1. One question per cue: how much energy? Press 1–4. A guess from the cue's speed and line count is shown, but it is never applied by itself.
  2. "Never auto" (N) keeps a cue for your hands only: "DROP" becomes the FLASH. Cues that can reach the audience are left out, and the screen says why.
  3. Each bin has a palette. The co-pilot steps colour only inside it.
  4. The vibes are built from the bins, with the rule shown, and thin bins are flagged.
  5. Accept makes 5 ordinary vibes, which the Vibe editor can still change (undo removes them).
