D3 · trust comes from limits you can read
  1. One row per energy: how often it hits, picks, accents, moves zones and steps colour. The ENERGY fader chooses the row.
  2. What it may change is a short list of things the engine already does (pool picks, zone maps, reaction depth, colour).
  3. What it may never do is fixed and safety-red: audience, blackout, FLASH, strobe above 3 Hz, safety zones.
  4. "When you play" sets how long your press keeps a lane, and what FREEZE holds.
  5. The log lists every change as AUTO, YOU, HELD or SAFETY, with its reason. You can filter and search it.
  6. Open an entry to see the checks behind it, and tune the rule from there ("Do this less").
