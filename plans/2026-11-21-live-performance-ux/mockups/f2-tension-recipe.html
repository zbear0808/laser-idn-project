F2 notes
  1. Four strong presets, one per kind of build. Most people never leave this row; the vibe picks one for you.
  2. Teach by playing: KEEP the last 8 bars of your pedal and pads, and they become a recipe fitted to the phrase.
  3. Eight lanes became three (shape, lift, shine). Experts can still open all eight; caps stay the vibe's.
  4. The drop is the kit's DROP voice (G). After it: KEEP a loop (G), hand over to the music (L), or decay.
  5. The music only adds: it catches the real drop, adds charge and drives sparkle (r08 #3). Off = the ring.
  6. Ranges per section come from the show file: how deep a build may go, and where the top 20% opens (J, M).
