G1 notes
  1. Minute 1: hit any pad. Early hits wait for the beat; up to 40 ms late still counts (r06 #1, r09 game feel 5–6).
  2. Each pad's chip says who plays that voice. Leave it alone and it follows its instrument in the track, else your loop, else the clock. Hit it and it's yours for 2 bars (r07 #10, r08 #4).
  3. KEEP saves 2 bars of a voice. Here the music's kick is kept, so the breakdown keeps its pulse.
  4. Hold DROP or the pedal to build. Hold time, pressure, clean hits and the music's riser fill the charge. Let go and it lands on 33.1 (r07 #17, r06 #22).
  5. Past a felt 70% click, a pad ratchets 1/16 as motion, never as flashes (r07 #4, r06 #19).
