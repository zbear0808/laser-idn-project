G2 notes
  1. One sheet shows the whole kit: each pad's look, which lasers its centre and edge play, what it follows in the music, and its clock fallback (r07 #5, r08 #4).
  2. Following is set per voice: the stem or a band-pass slice, a threshold and pull-to-grid, and how long your hit keeps the voice before it hands back.
  3. The timeline is the chip order made visible: music, then your hit for 2 bars, then the KEEP layer through the break, then music again.
  4. One kit per vibe, with the same pad places, so the hands never relearn (r07 §4 #5). NEXT and DROP never loop.
