L2 notes
  1. Start from a preset. They are sparse on purpose, so the rig doesn't look like a visualizer (r08 §5). The bars show which lanes are up.
  2. One row per lane, five columns: what it hears, what it drives, how it falls back, what the pedal does to it, and how far it may go per section.
  3. The ladder is the same on every lane: stem → band-pass → LOOP (a KEEP of the last 2 bars) → CLOCK. Vocals and lead have no fake: they rest.
  4. "In a build" is the tension recipe, split by lane. The pedal pushes all lanes at once.
  5. Ranges per section (Intro, Up, Drop, Down) come from the show file. Up caps every lane at 80 %; the top 20 % opens at the drop (r06 #22).
  6. Learn listens, scores each lane against the grid, and proposes. It never changes a link by itself.
