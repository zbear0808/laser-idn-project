L3 notes
  1. The faders are the instrument: one per lane, ATOM-style one-to-one (r07 #10). The 80 % detent and the locked top 20 % keep the peak for the drop (Flight Deck, r06 #22).
  2. Four lane pads, for the lanes that have moments: kick, snare, hats, vocal. Bass and lead are levels, so they live on their faders. Punches use the forgiving window (x1).
  3. A throw is a held quasimode on zones A–D: it lasts while held and lands back on the bar.
  4. The pedal is F's tension handle, laid over the music's own build. Every input also lights its pad or switch.
