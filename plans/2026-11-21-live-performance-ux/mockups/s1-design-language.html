3 · What a press does, in the corner

▶ Shotplays, then ends by itself (a HIT that decays after its beats; a Punch reaction)
▮ Holdon while your finger is down; let go and the look comes back (FLASH, Strobe, Swell)
⟳ Latchpress on, press off (BLACKOUT, a sustained bed)
⏱ On the barwaits for the next beat or bar; the pad flashes until it lands (vibe change, zone map)
chipsunder the name: what else the press moves (size bright A→B)

4 · Honest laser tiles

Tiles sit in rig order, left to right. A white ring means "this laser is emitting right now", after intensity, blackout, zones and safety. Everything else says why it is dark.

5 · The status band (readable from the room)

● LIVE · 4 of 4 lasers · 128.0 BPM
▲ DEGRADED · Center not answering · 3 of 4 lasers
■ BLACKOUT · press BLACKOUT again to bring the light back
🔒 SHOW LOCK · only the pads and faders work · hold 2 s to unlock

6 · Zones, and things the app did by itself

Zones are letters with a colour; a dashed chip is a zone with nothing in it. Anything the app changed on its own (a beat chase, a pool pick, an auto-advance) carries an AUTO tag and a one-line reason.

ABCD AUTOVibe → Peak: 32 bars in Build and a 2-beat gap
