Flash limiter · at most 3 full-field flashes in any second

Photosensitivity guidance. At 128 BPM, quarter notes (2.1 Hz) pass; eighth notes (4.3 Hz) do not, so an eighth-note strobe request is played as motion (a beam bounce) instead. Above 4 Hz, sync can't be judged anyway, so fast detail is treated as texture.
