# 02 — VJ and DJ software: performance features worth learning from

Research date: 2026-11-21. Topic: VJ tools (Resolume, VDMX, Synesthesia, Millumin, TouchDesigner, TouchOSC / Open Stage Control, MXWendler, Magic), DJ tools (rekordbox, Serato, Traktor, Engine/CDJ, djay, Pioneer DJM mixers) and automatic light shows for DJs (SoundSwitch, rekordbox Lighting, Engine Lighting). Pain point IDs P1–P8 are from the brief.

## 1. Summary

VJ and DJ tools have already solved most of what this app struggles with. They solve it with a small set of repeated ideas. Every trigger has an explicit **trigger style**: latch, toggle or hold. A **quantise** setting makes presses land on the beat. **One knob per channel** has a centre-off detent. **Macro dials** move many parameters at once, each within its own range. There is a hard split between **"prepared" and "live"**: a cue channel, a preview monitor, "silent edition". Live output gets a **colour you can see from across the room**: the CDJ jog ring turns red when that deck can be heard. **Momentary effects return to where the music would have been** (slip mode). Automatic lighting tools (SoundSwitch, rekordbox Lighting) add three more: **fallback loops that cycle every N bars**, **"AUTO" buttons that clear manual overrides**, and **looks that only touch the parameters they declare, letting the rest pass "Thru"**. The common complaints point the same way. Users hate hidden override layers, auto-analysis that makes everything look the same, mode state shown in one place but not another, and settings split across two screens.

## 2. Products

### Resolume Arena / Avenue (VJ)
- Layout: a grid of layers (rows) and columns. Clicking a column fires every clip in it ([manual: clips](https://resolume.com/support/en/clips)). Decks are pages of clips ([R4 manual](https://resolume.com/download/Resolume-4-Manual-English.pdf)).
- Trigger styles per clip, with a composition-wide default: **Normal** (restart on re-press), **Toggle** (second press stops it), **Piano** (plays only while held). Clip Target can be "Own / Active / Free layer". Free layer + Piano lets you play "chords" of clips ([clips](https://resolume.com/support/en/clips)).
- **Beat Snap** makes a clip wait for the next beat, bar, 2 bars or 4 bars. It can be set per clip or "Composition determined" ([clips](https://resolume.com/support/en/clips)).
- You can **select a clip without triggering it** by clicking its name handle instead of its thumbnail ([clips](https://resolume.com/support/en/clips)). A beginner guide calls the two click targets "same clip, two different click targets, and the difference matters a lot mid-set" ([VJ Academy](https://vjacademy.info/resolume-arena-guide)).
- **Autopilot** plays next / previous / random / a specific clip after N loops, or after N seconds or N beats. It can be set per clip, layer, column or composition ([clips](https://resolume.com/support/en/clips), [autopilot](https://resolume.com/support/en/autopilot)).
- BPM: type it in, **Tap**, then **Resync** on the "1". A blue square runs round the BPM display and outlines every 16 beats so you can check the phrase. +/- nudge buttons ([BPM](https://resolume.com/support/en/bpm)).
- Parameter animation: Timeline, BPM Sync, Clip Position, Audio FFT (L/M/H band buttons, Gain, Fall). Envelopes reshape any input, including MIDI/OSC ([parameter animation](https://resolume.com/support/parameter-animation), [envelopes](https://resolume.com/support/envelopes)). "Clip/Composition Beats" sets a parameter to max on each beat, then slides it down. The manual suggests using it on Scale for a beat "jump" and on Opacity for a BPM strobe ([R4 manual](https://resolume.com/download/Resolume-4-Manual-English.pdf)).
- **Dashboard**: 8 dials per composition, layer and clip. Drag any parameter onto a dial. Many parameters per dial, each with its own in/out range and invert ([dashboard](https://resolume.com/support/dashboard), [R4 manual](https://resolume.com/download/Resolume-4-Manual-English.pdf)).
- Crossfader: layers opt into bus A or B. Side buttons behave as Jump, Jump & Return, Cut (snaps back on release, "piano") or Fade (4 beats). Composition controls: **X** ejects all, **B** bypasses output, and the **M** slider "will show red when not set to 100%" ([composition](https://resolume.com/support/composition)).
- With Pioneer integration, a "Fader" toggle hides a video layer until its track can actually be heard ([Arena 7 manual](https://audioeffetti.com/product/documents/RES/ARENA7X2EDU-01.pdf)).
- Advanced Output (screens, slices): another researcher covers this.

### VDMX
- Modular windows. The **Control Surface** plugin builds custom panels of sliders, buttons, menus and colour wheels. It has "Custom UI" and "Edit UI" modes on a grid ([tutorial](https://vdmx.vidvox.net/tutorials/customizing-the-layout-of-a-control-surface-plugin)).
- Data sources: LFO (sine, random, bezier, in seconds or **measures** when locked to a Clock), Step Sequencer, Audio Analysis, Clock with tap or MIDI clock ([plugins](https://docs.vidvox.net/vdmx/vdmx_plugins)). You right-click-drag from a source to a control. A red arrow shows the link ([UI controls](https://docs.vidvox.net/vdmx/vdmx_ui_controls)).
- Buttons can be toggles, and can be "mutually exclusive with group name" (radio groups) ([blog walkthrough, Japanese](https://saikuro0526.hatenablog.com/entry/2017/02/21/220459)).

### Synesthesia
- Three control banks: **Scene controls** (per scene, stored in presets), **Meta controls** (global: Master, colour, mirror, Reactivity, Audio Speed, Limit Colors) and Media. Each bank has Lock, Default, Random and Undo ([controls](https://app.synesthesia.live/docs/manual/controls.html)).
- **Master brightness is deliberately not stored in presets** ([controls](https://app.synesthesia.live/docs/manual/controls.html)).
- **FavSlots**: 10 one-click preset slots above the controls, MIDI-mappable. The gold first slot is the scene's start look ([v1.16 notes](https://www.synesthesia.live/public-release/changelog/1.16.3.3.html), [presets](https://app.synesthesia.live/docs/manual/presets.html)). Playlists hold scene + preset pairs with countdowns. "Auto-Transition" moves on at a breakdown or drop ([presets](https://app.synesthesia.live/docs/manual/presets.html)).
- Setup is small. Pick an audio device and you are done. The meter shows green for signal, red for none, yellow for clipping ([audio FAQ](https://getsynesthesia.com/docs/faq/audio.html)). The engine publishes normalised features to every scene: Level, Hits, Presence, OnBeat, BPM and **BPMConfidence** per band ([audio uniforms](https://app.synesthesia.live/docs/ssf/audio_uniforms.html)).
- Hotkeys on a selected control: D = default, R = random, P = preset value, L = lock. "Try pressing them in sync with the beat" ([hotkeys](https://app.synesthesia.live/docs/manual/control_hotkeys.html)). MIDI output drives controller LEDs and motor faders ([MIDI FAQ](https://app.synesthesia.live/docs/faq/midi_osc.html)).
- A separate "Performance mode": **not verified**. I found no doc page by that name.

### Millumin
- The Dashboard is a sequencer of columns. SPACE fires the next column ([dashboard](http://help.millumin.com/docs/playback/dashboard)).
- **Silent Edition**: while it is on, edits are not seen on the outputs. The board playing on the outputs is highlighted orange ([dashboard](http://help.millumin.com/docs/playback/dashboard)). In beta: "preview-and-select the next column" and skip columns ([beta](https://help.millumin.com/docs/general/beta-version)).
- Interactions mode turns every mappable element green. Click one, then press a key to bind it. The model is input → transformer → action, with optional feedback ([interactions](http://help.millumin.com/docs/connect/interactions)). The Monitor has global masters for video, audio and DMX, plus manual control of transitions ([monitor](https://help.millumin.com/docs/connect/monitor/)).

### TouchDesigner
- **Perform Mode** renders only one window: outputs plus your own control panel. F1 enters it, Esc exits. A project can start in Perform Mode. With Privacy set you cannot leave it ([Perform Mode](https://derivative.ca/UserGuide/Perform_Mode)).

### TouchOSC / Open Stage Control / Lemur
- TouchOSC buttons: **Momentary, Toggle Release, Toggle Press**, with press and release events separately enabled ([TouchOSC controls](https://hexler.net/touchosc/manual/controls)). "Local feedback off" means the button only changes when the host confirms it ([Mk1 reference](http://hexler.net/touchosc-mk1/manual/controls-reference)).
- Open Stage Control buttons: toggle / push / momentary / tap. They also have **doubleTap** (needs two taps) and **decoupled** (display follows the received state, not the touch) ([properties](https://openstagecontrol.ammd.net/docs/widgets/properties-reference/)).
- Lemur: not researched (not verified).

### MXWendler
- Preload (media) and Playlist (show flow) are kept separate on purpose, so media can be swapped without rebuilding the show ([software](https://www.mxwendler.net/en/products/software.html)). In the playlist, a row is a cue. Only one cell per column can be active ([manual 6.0](https://www.mxwendler.net/files/uploads/00_MXWENDLER/Manual_MXW_6.0_En.pdf)). The playing row shows green ([4.2 manual, French](https://manualzilla.com/doc/6367020/mxwendler-4.2)).
- Their Stage Recorder has a **two-stage soft stop**: the first stop rewinds and pauses, the second unloads. "Save & Restart" appears in red only when a restart is really needed ([Stage Recorder UI](https://wiki.mxwendler.net/index.php?title=Stage_Recorder_User_Interface)).

### Magic Music Visuals
- **Global Parameters**: name a value once and link it to many module parameters across all scenes ([user guide](https://magicmusicvisuals.com/downloads/Magic_UsersGuide.html)). Audio, MIDI and OSC features arrive as 0–1, with "modifiers" to rescale them ([user guide](https://magicmusicvisuals.com/downloads/Magic_UsersGuide.html)).

### rekordbox performance pads
- 8 or 16 pads, switched by mode: HOT CUE, PAD FX, SLICER, BEAT JUMP, BEAT LOOP, KEYBOARD, KEY SHIFT, SEQ CALL, ACTIVE CENSOR ([manual 7.2.18](https://cdn.rekordbox.com/files/20260807093645/rekordbox7.2.18_manual_EN.pdf)). Pad Editor offers templates, USER 1–8 and a LIGHTING template ([Pad Editor](https://cdn.rekordbox.com/files/20251202174704/rekordbox7.2.8_pad_editor_operation_guide_EN.pdf)).
- **Pad FX**: the effect is on while the pad is held and lit. Two banks of 16. Several pads can be held at once, but "if an effect has different beats, only the last performance pad you pressed turns on" ([manual 7.2.18](https://cdn.rekordbox.com/files/20260807093645/rekordbox7.2.18_manual_EN.pdf)).
- **Release FX** pad: HOLD ON/OFF picks momentary or latched ([manual 7.0](https://cdn.rekordbox.com/files/20240509141437/rekordbox7.0.0_manual_EN.pdf)). Pressing it turns off the other Pad FX ([manual 6.3](https://cdn.rekordbox.com/files/20201111111952/rekordbox6.3.0_manual_EN.pdf)).
- **Slip mode**: while you loop, scratch or hold a hot cue, the track keeps playing silently underneath. A line on the waveform shows that hidden playhead. On release, playback carries on from it ([manual 7.2.8](https://cdn.rekordbox.com/files/20251202174516/rekordbox7.2.8_manual_EN.pdf)).

### Pioneer DJM mixers and CDJs
- **Colour FX**: six buttons pick one effect for every channel. Each channel has its own COLOR knob. Centre is off, left and right give different flavours ([Garnish](https://edu.garnishmusicproduction.com/dj-mixer-effects-colour-fx-beat-fx-slip-mode)). The DJM-A9's **Center Lock** stops the knob at centre so a fast twist cannot overshoot into the opposite side ([DJM-A9](https://www.pioneerdj.com/en/product/dj-mixers/djm-a9)).
- **Beat FX**: one effect at a time. Pick a channel and a beat fraction, then hit a single ON button ([Club Ready](https://www.clubreadydjschool.com/tribe-talk/getting-started/beat-vs-color-fx)). BPM can be AUTO or TAP. If no BPM is detected, the last value is shown flashing ([DJM-850 manual, Spanish](https://www.manualsdir.com/manuals/483493/pioneer-djm-850-s-djm-850-k.html?page=71)).
- **On Air Display**: a CDJ's jog ring turns red when its channel can be heard. That accounts for the channel fader, crossfader and isolator EQs ([Pioneer forum](https://forums.pioneerdj.com/hc/en-us/community/posts/203090629--SOLVED-White-and-Red-Jog-Wheel-Lights), [protocol notes](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Deep-Symmetry/dysentery/main/doc/modules/ROOT/pages/mixer_integration.adoc)). The CDJ-3000 jog ring also blinks when the track is about to end ([CDJ-3000 manual](https://prosoundonline.com/wp-content/uploads/CDJ-3000_manual_EN.pdf)).

### Traktor (Pro, Remix Decks, Kontrol F1)
- Each mixer channel has a **Cue** (headphones) button for preparing the next track ([Pro 4 manual](https://native-instruments.com/fileadmin/ni_media/downloads/manuals/traktor/Traktor-Pro-4-Manual-English-170724.pdf)). The **phase meter** is empty when the decks are in phase and grows left or right when they are not ([Traktor Play overview](https://docs.native-instruments.com/online-guides/traktor-play-user-guide/en/traktor-play-overview)). The Stripe flashes red at the track-end warning time ([basic tutorials](https://docs.native-instruments.com/ni-tech-manuals/traktor-pro-manual/en/basic-usage-tutorials)).
- Two-button mouse trick: hold both buttons while changing a parameter, then pick which button to release first. That decides whether you keep the new value or go back to the old one ([Traktor Play](https://docs.native-instruments.com/ni-tech-manuals/traktor-play-user-guide/en/getting-started-with-traktor-play)).
- **Mixer FX**: one knob per channel, filter in both directions, centre off. Four preselected effects plus Filter. The FX button colour shows which effect is active ([MX2 guide](https://docs.native-instruments.com/online-guides/traktor-mx2-user-guide/en/traktor-mx2-overview)).
- **F1**: a 4×4 pad grid. Pad colours match the software. Bright = playing, dim = loaded but idle ([F1 manual](https://docs.native-instruments.com/pdf-guides/traktor/traktor_kontrol_f1_manual_english.pdf)). Only one sample plays per column, and hitting another in the same column replaces it ([F1 manual p.32](https://www.manua.ls/native-instruments/traktor-kontrol-f1/manual?p=32)). Each sample is Gate (hold) or Latch ([Loop Rituals review, German](https://looprituals.de/ni-traktor-2-5-remix-decks-im-test)). The Browse button is "the only blue-lit Mode button… so that you don't accidentally load a Sample… during a session" ([F1 manual](https://docs.native-instruments.com/pdf-guides/traktor/traktor_kontrol_f1_manual_english.pdf)).

### Serato DJ Pro
- Stems: pads 1–4 mute Vocal / Melody / Bass / Drums (blue). Pads 5–8 are Stem FX (orange). Waveform colours change as stems are removed ([Using Stems](https://support.serato.com/hc/en-us/articles/5700921209615-Using-Stems), [hardware](https://support.serato.com/hc/en-us/articles/5700970589583-Controlling-Stems-with-your-DJ-hardware)). SLAB's "Combo Pad Mode" splits 16 pads into two 8-pad groups, each in a different mode ([SLAB guide](https://support.serato.com/hc/en-us/articles/17924955426319-SLAB-Quickstart-Guide-for-Serato-DJ-Pro)).

### Algoriddim djay
- The default view is "Classic Mode": two turntables and a crossfader ([iPad manual](https://download.algoriddim.com/manual/djay-ios-ipad-manual-loqual.pdf)). There is a streamlined **Starter Mode** ([press release](https://www.algoriddim.com/press_releases/447-algoriddim-unveils-djay-pro-5-with-next-generation-neural-mix-crossfader-fusion-and-fluid-beatgrid-)). **Crossfader Fusion** puts preset transition behaviours on the one crossfader ([press release](https://www.algoriddim.com/press_releases/447-algoriddim-unveils-djay-pro-5-with-next-generation-neural-mix-crossfader-fusion-and-fluid-beatgrid-)). **Mute FX** adds an echo tail instead of a hard cut when a stem is muted ([Neural Mix](https://help.algoriddim.com/user-manual/djay-ios/neural-mix/overview)).

### SoundSwitch
- Two modes: Edit and Performance. Lights follow the DJ software's track, playhead, beat grid and fader position. If you loop the track, the lights loop too ([user guide](https://cdn.inmusicbrands.com/soundswitch/files/User%20Guide.pdf), [what is SoundSwitch](https://support.soundswitch.com/en/support/solutions/articles/69000847410-soundswitch-what-is-soundswitch-)).
- **Autoloops** are 8–128-bar light clips used for tracks with no script. They cycle in beat sync, e.g. a colour change every 8 bars. There are 4 banks of 32. The advice is to keep them simple, "solid color loops… slow intensity fades" ([Autoloops](https://support.soundswitch.com/en/support/solutions/articles/69000847100-soundswitch-autoloops-explained)). Since 2.9 you can trigger one over a scripted track; it plays for its length and then hands back. Active loops show a progress indicator ([2.9](https://support.soundswitch.com/en/support/solutions/articles/69000858487-soundswitch-autoloop-improvements-in-soundswitch-2-9)).
- **Autoscript** uses presets plus **fixture categories** ("Wash Primary / Secondary") rather than per-fixture wiring ([autoscript autoloops](https://support.soundswitch.com/en/support/solutions/articles/69000844240-soundswitch-how-to-autoscript-autoloops)). **Attribute Cues** (gobo, prism) are edited once, and every script that uses them updates. That is meant for venue-specific changes ([attribute cues](https://support.soundswitch.com/en/support/solutions/articles/69000828942-soundswitch-controlling-gobo-prism-more-with-attribute-cues)).
- **Static Looks** (2.11): several can be active at once. Each fixture parameter follows **last-triggered priority**, and parameters set to **Thru** pass through from the script. A new **Active Static Looks pane** lists what is on, in priority order, with mute and clear-all buttons ([Static Looks 2.11](https://support.soundswitch.com/en/support/solutions/articles/69000881393-soundswitch-static-look-improvements-in-2-11)).
- Crossfader modes: Blend (blue + red = purple), Cut (switches at 50 %), Scratch (switches at 90 %), Upfader only. Optional "Loop Auto Strobe" fires when a loop is shorter than half a beat ([preferences](https://support.soundswitch.com/en/support/solutions/articles/69000847088-performance-mode-preferences-explained)).
- Control One hardware has: Autoloop bank buttons, colour overrides, position overrides (SHIFT), WHITE / BLACK / UV and **WHITE HOLD / BLACK HOLD / UV HOLD**, strobe, smoke, BPM TAP, an "Override scripted tracks" button and an intensity touch strip with groups ([Control One guide](https://cdn.inmusicbrands.com/soundswitch/files/User%20Guide%20Control%20One.pdf)).
- "SoundSwitch cannot discover and patch your lighting fixtures automatically… this only needs to be done once" ([user guide](https://cdn.inmusicbrands.com/soundswitch/files/User%20Guide.pdf)).

### rekordbox Lighting
- Three steps: assign fixtures, run phrase analysis, play. Phrases are Intro / Up / Down / Chorus / Bridge / Verse / Outro. **Mood** is HIGH / MID / LOW. **Bank** is the style (Cool, Natural, Hot, …) ([lighting guide](https://cdn.rekordbox.com/files/20241216130922/rekordbox7.0.7_lighting_operation_guide_EN.pdf)).
- The performance panel shows Mood/Bank, the current phrase, Colour, Strobe (Fast / Mid / Slow / Off), Brightness and BLACK OUT. **Each override has an AUTO button to hand control back** ([lighting guide](https://cdn.rekordbox.com/files/20241216130922/rekordbox7.0.7_lighting_operation_guide_EN.pdf)).
- **Ambient mode** runs lights with no track playing, and can start automatically when all decks stop ([lighting guide](https://cdn.rekordbox.com/files/20241216130922/rekordbox7.0.7_lighting_operation_guide_EN.pdf)). **Venue** stores a fixture patch ([FAQ](https://rekordbox.com/en/support/faq/lighting-7)). **Delay compensation** runs from −500 to +500 ms. Pads work as "press to turn on, press again to reset" ([6.0 guide](https://cdn.rekordbox.com/files/20200413101417/rekordbox6.0.0_lighting_operation_guide_EN.pdf)).

### Engine Lighting
- Runs on the DJ hardware, with no laptop. "Raise the fader, press play, and enjoy the show." The touchscreen triggers colour overrides, strobe, UV, smoke, Autoloops and Static Looks. Moving heads follow a finger swipe ([Engine Lighting](http://enginedj.com/enginelighting)). Basic mode (Hue / Nanoleaf) is free. Advanced mode (DMX) needs SoundSwitch ([Engine support](https://support.enginedj.com/support/solutions/articles/69000883322-denon-dj-prime-4-g2-introduction-to-engine-lighting)).

## 3. Patterns worth stealing

1. **Explicit trigger style per pad, with one global default.** Source: Resolume Normal/Toggle/Piano plus "Composition determined"; Traktor Gate/Latch; rekordbox Release FX HOLD ON/OFF; TouchOSC button types. Pain points: P1, P3, P7. Idea: every cue or reaction cell shows a corner glyph: ▶ latch, ⏻ toggle, ✋ hold. A Perform-tab setting "Default press = Hold / Latch". A right-click on a pad overrides it for that pad only.

2. **Beat Snap with a visible "armed" state.** Source: Resolume Beat Snap (beat, bar, 2, 4 bars); Traktor F1 quantise. Pain points: P1, P6. Idea: a pressed-but-waiting pad shows a pulsing amber outline. A ring around it fills until the next beat or bar, then the pad goes solid. A snap selector (Off / Beat / Bar) sits next to the BPM readout. HIT stays unquantised by default.

3. **Reactions as named beat envelopes.** Source: Resolume "Clip Beats" (max on the beat, then slide down; Scale = jump, Opacity = strobe); Synesthesia `syn_OnBeat` / `syn_Hits`. Pain point: P3. Idea: each tap reaction draws a tiny curve on its pad (punch ▲ decays over ½ beat; pump ▲▼ over 1 beat). The user picks a shape and a length in beats, not attack and release numbers.

4. **Macro dial with per-target ranges, shown on demand.** Source: Resolume Dashboard (8 dials, many parameters each, in/out range, invert); Magic Global Parameters. Pain points: P1, P3, P7. Idea: holding or hovering the Morph fader opens an overlay listing what it moves. For example: "Size 80→120 %, Hue +0→+60°, Scatter 0→0.4", each with a mini range bar.

5. **One bipolar knob with a centre detent and centre lock.** Source: DJM Colour FX (centre off); DJM-A9 Center Lock; Traktor Mixer FX. Pain points: P1, P8. Idea: make Morph bipolar. Left = calmer, right = wilder, centre = no change. It snaps to centre and does not overshoot. On a mouse, double-click resets it to centre.

6. **Pick the effect once, then switch it on per channel.** Source: DJM Beat FX (one effect, channel select, one ON button); Traktor Mixer FX select 1–4. Pain points: P5, P7. Idea: a "Zone FX" strip. Choose one effect (e.g. Strobe) from a short list. Four large A / B / C / D ON buttons then apply it to those zones. Only the ON buttons are live controls.

7. **Two click targets: prepare vs fire.** Source: Resolume name handle (select) vs thumbnail (fire); Traktor Cue channel; Millumin "select next column". Pain points: P2, P6. Idea: clicking a cue label loads it into an amber **NEXT** slot with its own preview. Clicking the thumbnail or pressing GO fires it. Live has a red border, next has an amber border, nothing else is coloured.

8. **On-air colour that follows what the audience really sees.** Source: CDJ jog ring red only when the channel is audible after faders, crossfader and EQ. Pain point: P6. Idea: each projector thumbnail gets a red ring only when it is actually emitting. That takes Intensity, blackout, zone mask, safety zones and an empty zone into account. Otherwise the ring is white. "Selected but dark" must never look like live.

9. **Warn when master output is below full.** Source: Resolume M slider turns red below 100 %; Synesthesia keeps Master out of presets. Pain points: P6, P8. Idea: a top bar banner, "OUTPUT 40 %", in amber whenever Intensity is under 100 %. Intensity is never saved in vibes or cues.

10. **Slip: momentary moves return to where the show would be.** Source: rekordbox slip mode (hidden playhead shown as a line); djay Mute FX echo tail. Pain point: P3. Idea: while FLASH or a punch is held, the bed chase keeps running underneath. A faint "ghost" step marker on the step grid shows this. On release the output rejoins the bed in phase.

11. **A "release" button that clears effects without going dark.** Source: rekordbox Release FX turns off the other Pad FX. Pain points: P8, P3. Idea: a CLEAN action (long-press BLACKOUT, or its own key) drops all latched reactions and zone FX back to the bare vibe. It shows a one-second "cleared 3" toast.

12. **Fallback loops that cycle by bars, plus an idle look.** Source: SoundSwitch Autoloops (switch every N bars, keep them simple); Resolume Autopilot by beats; rekordbox Ambient mode when decks stop; Synesthesia auto-transition at drops. Pain points: P1, P2. Idea: each vibe gets an "Auto-rotate bed every 8 / 16 / 32 bars" toggle, with a bar countdown on the bed pad. A global "Idle look" plays when there is no beat or input for N seconds.

13. **Every override has an AUTO return.** Source: rekordbox Lighting panel (Colour, Strobe and Mood each have AUTO); SoundSwitch "Override scripted tracks". Pain points: P1, P7. Idea: a manual colour or vibe override lights a small **AUTO** pill next to that control. One press of AUTO hands control back. A global "AUTO ALL" sits beside BLACKOUT.

14. **Looks that declare what they touch; the rest passes Thru.** Source: SoundSwitch Static Looks (per-parameter Thru, last-triggered priority, Active Looks pane with mute and clear). Pain points: P3, P6, P7. Idea: a reaction or FX set lists the parameters it touches (chips: Size, Colour). Add an "Active now" stack, newest on top, e.g. "Pump → Brightness · Step colour → Hue". Every row has mute and remove buttons.

15. **Roles and categories, not per-device wiring; venue = patch only.** Source: SoundSwitch fixture categories ("Wash Primary / Secondary"); rekordbox Venue; MXWendler Preload vs Playlist split; SoundSwitch Attribute Cues edited once for the venue. Pain points: P4, P5. Idea: tag projectors with roles (Main, Side-L, Side-R, Accent). Cues and zone maps target roles. A "Venue" file holds only the projector list, order, roles and safety zones. Switching venue never touches cues.

16. **Three-state pads, same colour on screen and on hardware.** Source: Traktor F1 (bright = playing, dim = loaded, colours match the software); Serato blue vs orange pad families. Pain points: P1, P6. Idea: every pad is **off** (empty), **dim** (ready) or **bright** (live). The pad's colour family is the same on screen and on MIDI controller LEDs: roles one hue, reactions another, safety red.

17. **Feedback from engine state, not from the click.** Source: TouchOSC "local feedback off"; Open Stage Control "decoupled"; Synesthesia and Millumin MIDI feedback. Pain point: P6. Idea: a pad lights only when the engine confirms the action. If a press does nothing (empty pool, zone with no lasers), the pad flashes grey with "no target" for 1 s.

18. **Guards on dangerous controls.** Source: Open Stage Control doubleTap; MXWendler two-stage soft stop; rekordbox Eject/Load Lock; Traktor F1 blue Browse button. Pain point: P8. Idea: un-blanking after BLACKOUT needs a double-tap or a 1 s hold. Show Lock is hold-to-unlock. Any control that can start audience-scan content uses a unique colour (e.g. blue) that nothing else uses.

19. **A separate perform window and a silent-edit mode.** Source: TouchDesigner Perform Mode (F1/Esc, can start in it, can be locked); Millumin Silent Edition (edits hidden from output, live board in orange). Pain points: P1, P7, P6. Idea: F1 opens a full-screen Perform view with only pads, faders and thumbnails. "Silent edit" lets you change a cue while the output stays frozen on the last state, with an orange "EDITING — output frozen" frame.

20. **Tempo honesty: tap, resync, phrase marker, confidence, latency nudge.** Source: Resolume blue square and 16-beat outline; DJM BPM flashes when not detected; Synesthesia `syn_BPMConfidence`; rekordbox ±500 ms delay compensation. Pain points: P1, P6. Idea: the BPM box shows a 4-step beat dot and a 16-beat phrase ring. It flashes when the tempo is a guess. A "laser latency" nudge (ms) sits in setup.

## 4. Anti-patterns and complaints

- **Hidden override layers.** In SoundSwitch, a fixture with an empty track follows the master track. Users see "all of the lights are running, all of the time" ([Engine DJ forum](https://community.enginedj.com/t/autoloops-all-lights-always-on/54542), [main track question](https://community.enginedj.com/t/main-track-question/64072)). (P3, P6)
- **Auto-analysis flattens everything.** "Every song I load… is analysed as cool high mood, so every single song looks the exact same" ([Pioneer forum](https://forums.pioneerdj.com/hc/en-us/community/posts/12431633468569-Rekordbox-lighting-Take-some-leaves-out-of-soundswitch-s-book)). The same list says lights "often strobe when inappropriate" and go to 0 % at outros ([2023 list](https://forums.pioneerdj.com/hc/en-us/community/posts/21327404143769-Rekordbox-Lighting-2023-Future)). For SoundSwitch: "the song analysis is terrible" and lights hit unwanted positions like a wedding cake ([Engine DJ forum](https://community.enginedj.com/t/what-bothers-me-most-about-soundswitch/67266)). (P2, P8)
- **No audition before applying.** "How can we decide what macros to use if we can't audition them first?" ([Pioneer forum](https://forums.pioneerdj.com/hc/en-us/community/posts/360026473752-RB-5-4-0-Lighting-Mode-7-more-bugs)). Phrase editing should be as quick as setting hot cues "in less than 60 seconds" ([Pioneer forum](https://forums.pioneerdj.com/hc/en-us/community/posts/360025058571-Rekordbox-Lighting-Not-quite-there)). (P2, P6)
- **Mode state out of sync between screen and hardware.** On the DDJ-200, the pad mode lives in a bar at the bottom of the rekordbox screen and does not follow the deck GUI ([Pioneer forum](https://forums.pioneerdj.com/hc/en-us/community/posts/4554078430489-Help-Performance-Pads-stuck-on-Hot-Cue-Mode-on-Rekordbox-using-DDJ-200)). In djay, the UI does not change to match hardware pad modes on most controllers ([Algoriddim forum](https://community.algoriddim.com/t/ddj-rev5-ui-not-changing-to-match-pad-modes/34182)). An unexpected default pad mode made a DJ "turn off one of the stems when attempting to hit a hot cue" ([VirtualDJ forum](https://virtualdj.com/forums/238048/VirtualDJ_Technical_Support/Stems_default_pad_mode_on_DDJ-1000_Deck_3___4.html)). (P1, P6)
- **One setting in two places.** Resolume's own manual admits external FFT must be set "in two places… we could at least put them a bit closer together" ([parameter animation](https://resolume.com/support/en/7/parameter-animation)). (P4)
- **Layout-dependent surprises.** Resolume's crossfader gives "unexpected results" if unassigned layers sit between assigned ones ([composition](https://resolume.com/support/composition)). A troubleshooting guide's first checks for a dark output are the M slider, layer V slider and Bypass ([enotion guide](https://note.com/enablestudio/n/nb584e9a1392b?hl=en)): three silent ways to be black. (P6, P7)
- **Tempo sync pain.** Tap tempo "drifts off after a while so you'll be constantly adjusting" ([r/vjing](https://www.reddit.com/r/vjing/comments/1kr3dby/how_can_vj_and_dj_sync_bpm_resolume/)). With Ableton Link, a tempo change in the VJ app changes the DJ's tempo ([Zero To VJ](https://zerotovj.com/using-ableton-link-to-sync-resolume-6-with-djs)). Link phase can land one beat off the downbeat ([VirtualDJ forum](https://virtualdj.com/forums/244465/VirtualDJ_Technical_Support/Ableton_link___VDJ_shifted_by_one_beat.html)). (P1)
- **Bindings that silently stop firing.** Millumin timecode interactions "only work once" until the transformer is set to none ([Millumin forum](https://forum.millumin.com/discussion/2413/ableton-timecode-millumin)). A TouchOSC push button sends twice per touch, and the workaround is a toggle with feedback off ([forum](https://forums.musicplayer.com/topic/165837-touchosc-and-mainstage-question)). (P6)
- **Coupled features.** A CDJ user wants the red on-air ring but not the automatic master-deck switch that comes with it ([Pioneer forum](https://forums.pioneerdj.com/hc/en-us/community/posts/26089762020121-On-air-display-without-automatic-switching-the-master-deck)). (P6, P7)
- **Automation without an obvious manual path.** "SS is purely automation only and has no 'manual' control"; "I just couldn't figure out what SS actually did" ([VirtualDJ forum](https://virtualdj.com/forums/232435/General_Discussion/SoundSwitch_Review_.html)). (P7)

## 5. Open questions for the app

1. Should presses be quantised to the beat by default? HIT may need to stay instant, with ACCENT and zone patterns snapped. Who decides: a global setting or each pad?
2. What is the default trigger style for reactions: hold (slip-style return) or latch? Is there one global default the user can see?
3. Is there a "NEXT" staging slot with a preview that does not emit, so a vibe change can be prepared and then committed?
4. What are the override priority rules: last-pressed wins per parameter (SoundSwitch), or a fixed order? How is the "Active now" stack shown in under 2 m of glance?
5. Do zones A–D act as "fixture categories"? Or do we also need projector roles (Main / Side / Accent) that survive venue changes?
6. What does "on air" mean per projector? Which inputs count: Intensity, blackout, safety zones, empty zone, network loss?
7. Where does tempo come from: tap, audio input, Ableton Link or MIDI clock? Who is the tempo leader, and do we show BPM confidence?
8. Should there be autopilot (bed rotation every N bars) and an idle look? How is it turned off at once?
9. Which MIDI controllers get LED feedback, and can screen and hardware colours be guaranteed to match?
10. Do we need per-projector latency compensation so beat flashes land with the sound?
11. Is Morph bipolar with a centre "no change", or unipolar? What is its saved default?
12. Which controls need a guard (double-tap or hold): un-blank, unlock, audience-scan cues, venue switch?
