# 05 — Rig setup and spatial mapping

Research date: 2026-11-21. Focus: P4 (rig setup), P5 (cues that don't need to know the rig), P8 (safety), with P6 (screen honesty).

## 1. Summary

The tools that make multi-output setup fast all split the work into three layers. First, **find and name the physical device**. They use a two-way "identify" (press in software and the device flashes; Q-SYS also works the other way, from the hardware). Second, **say where it is**. That means a map, a placement grid, or an order that the tool guesses and you confirm. Third, **let content target roles, not devices**. Examples are disguise Stage vs Feed, Resolume slices, BEYOND zone *names* and MAtricks wings and blocks. The consumer apps (Hue, Nanoleaf, Sonos) cut setup to minutes because placement gives instant feedback: Hue lights change colour as you drag them on the map. The pro audio apps (L-Acoustics, d&b R1, Shure WWB, Q-SYS) handle venue changes well. You prepare offline with placeholder devices. At the venue you match placeholders to found devices, and each device shows a fixed status word and colour. Laser software adds rules that fail closed. In BEYOND, output is off at launch, and content aimed at an unknown zone is blocked. LaserCube shows "keep-within" zones in green and "keep-out" zones in red. PASS hardware closes the shutter when anything is wrong. The main complaints are rerouting that can send hot beams into the audience, ticking the same projector in many places, outputs that swap order after a reboot, and placement maps whose effect users cannot predict.

## 2. Per product

### 2.1 Pangolin BEYOND / QuickShow (laser)

- **Projection zones** are the routing unit. A zone combines a projector choice, geometric correction, preview appearance, a 64×64 Beam Attenuation Map (BAM), "Also To", an effect, and advanced flags. BEYOND supports up to 40 projectors per PC. http://wiki.pangolin.com/doku.php?id=beyond:projection_zones-new
- **Recommended setup order:** set controller order, then projector settings, then zones. "Controllers and Projectors are sort of 'low-level layer'... Projection Zones use a higher level setup." (same URL)
- **Zones are identified by name, not index**, so content can move between rigs. "If the content uses a zone's name that does not exist, then output for this content will be blocked. This is done for safety reasons." An **Unresolved Zone Names** tab counts cues aimed at missing zones and lets you remap them or add an alias ("additional names"). (same URL)
- **Offline hardware is shown in red:** "When a listing is red, it is because there is no laser controller that is able to output to that projector." (same URL)
- **Identification by test pattern:** each zone's test pattern "also has an identifying number", and "View" can show the zone index that matches the number. Test patterns are **not shown automatically by default, "for safety reasons"**. (same URL)
- **Bulk creation:** the BEYOND 5.5 "Add" window creates many zones across many lasers and numbers them automatically. It can also delete the stock zones in the same step. Zone files can be exported and imported as CSV. Multi-zone editing works for geometry, BAM and preview. (same URL)
- **Projector ordering** uses list position ("Projector 1, Projector 2"), not serial number. https://wiki.pangolin.com/doku.php?id=beyond:projector_settings-new
- **Two ways to route cues:** tick zones in each cue's properties, or use "zone hot keys". The preview splits into 4, 9 or 16 cells laid out like the numeric keypad. Press a number to send the following cues there, and press 0 to return to "cue-preferred zones". Some cues opt out of rerouting. https://wiki.pangolin.com/doku.php?id=beyond:controlling_multiple_lasers and https://pangolin.com/blogs/news/understanding-zones-and-working-with-multiple-lasers
- **Preview mirroring:** a zone's preview can be set to "Audience Scanning Beams", with a vanishing point and "mirror output and position" for one controller that feeds both sides of a stage. (blog URL above)
- **Template rig (Performer Console):** a 40-laser busking zone file in 4 colour-coded groups (A orange, B teal, C aqua, D purple). Each group is named `L1 L2 L3 L4 R4 R3 R2 R1`, with "house left / operator's left" as Left and the smaller numbers on the outside. It includes recipes for 2, 4, 8, 12, 16 and 24 lasers. Live "flip" presets: symmetrical, linear, middle flipped, every 2, every 4. Live "selection orders": symmetrical, linear, up-down, left-right, clockwise, diagonal. Live-selected lasers "show up in white". Warning: "the names of zones are irrelevant, what matters is the numerical order... it's important to not delete zones from groups, or re-order them. If a zone remains unused, mute the zone." https://wiki.pangolin.com/doku.php?id=beyond:beyondperformerconsole and https://wiki.pangolin.com/doku.php?id=examples:zone-files
- **Output arming:** "laser output is always disabled by default, and it is always required for the laser operator to consciously activate laser output". Each editor also has its own "Show it now" button. http://wiki.pangolin.com/doku.php?id=beyond:enable_laser_output

### 2.2 LaserCube / LaserOS (laser)

- **Connection state is one button with three words:** `NO LASER` (not found), `LASER OFF` with a red power symbol (found, output off), `LASER ON`. If the cube is not armed, the app shows "Interlock is not enabled on cube" with the serial number. On the phone the button turns blue even when the cube isn't armed (a screen-honesty gap). https://wiki.laseros.com/docs/guides/ultra-mk2-pro-manual/
- **Projector Setup** has a drop-down of pages (Info, Image, Color Balance, Safety Zones) and a device list with a "Use This Device" switch. Info shows temperature, battery, interlock state, and DAC rate against the maximum. (same URL)
- **Safety zones:** with Invert off a zone is "keep-within" with a **green outline**. With Invert on it is "keep-out" with a **red outline**. "Where zones overlap, red has priority over green." The shapes include rectangle, ellipse, triangle, corners and diamond. In the web app, Preview projects the zone outline until you leave, or "about 5 seconds after the cube stops hearing from the page". (same URL)
- **Multi-cube:** each cube gets a tab "named by its model and the last 4 digits of its MAC address". In the Timeline, "give each cube its own layer". Cube Link uses one Master and many Slaves, each with a Group number (a 2-channel show over 4 cubes is 2 + 2). A slave that has found its master shows a purple status bar. (same URL) The Academy adds: "Every cube is its own laser... Each cube has its own key and interlock." https://wiki.laseros.com/academy/control-modes

### 2.3 MadMapper (video + laser)

- Laser outputs have test-pattern toggles: **Enable Scan Area, Enable Geometry, Enable Name, Enable Color, Enable Masks**. So the projector can draw its own name. Masks "protect cameras or audiences". A mask at low opacity (for example 10%) is described as a way to scan an audience at reduced brightness. Video outputs have Flip and Delay. "Disabling an active output during a show will immediately close its fullscreen projection." https://docs.madmapper.com/madmapper/6/6.-outputs

### 2.4 Resolume Arena Advanced Output (video)

- Screens are listed on the left. Right-click a screen to pick a physical output, and double-click to rename it. Selecting an output already in use sets the other screen back to virtual. A toggle per screen enables or disables it, and this is "very useful when identifying which screen is which". **Virtual screens** let you prepare before the venue and bind the real output later. https://resolume.com/support/en/screens
- **Identify Displays** overlays each output with "a numbered and coloured image". Resolume notes that Windows numbering ("3-1-2-8-9-11-10...") does not match the table order. **Show Test Card** shows colour bars, resolution and time. Ctrl/Cmd-Shift-D disables all outputs. https://resolume.com/support/en/output-setup
- Slices cut the composition "like a pie". "Match Output Shape" copies the output position back to the input. https://resolume.com/support/en/input-selection
- **Identify Slices** lights a slice in white on mouse-over. Combined with "Show in Output", this shows it on the real stage. https://www.resolume.com/support/en/modifiers

### 2.5 Millumin and disguise (video, brief)

- Millumin: "the `main screen` is clearly identified, so you do not select it by mistake". Each output has its own colour and delay in frames. Mapping can be done at canvas level so content and projection stay separate. https://help.millumin.com/docs/display/outputs and https://help.millumin.com/docs/display/mapping
- disguise keeps three levels apart: Timeline, Stage and Feed. "a change in the screen layout or output formatting does not require a change of content." https://s3.amazonaws.com/help.disguise.one/PublishedPDFs/Getting+Started+Guide.pdf You can switch between whole feed sets "at the click of a button". https://help.disguise.one/designer/ui/designer-user-interface
- **Head status colours:** green configured; red refresh mismatch; orange changed but not applied; blue Designer machine; grey not connected; flashing blue/grey applying. Each head has a latency of −30 to +30 frames, where "a negative value instead delays all other heads". https://help.disguise.one/designer/feed-outputs/head-configuration

### 2.6 grandMA3 (lighting)

- **Highlight / Lowlight / Solo:** "temporary visualization overrides... do not get stored into cues". Use them to "identify fixtures in a crowded rig, confirm selection order, build or troubleshoot groups". The workflow is: select a group, press Highlt, then press Next repeatedly to see the order. https://help.malighting.com/grandMA3/2.4/HTML/highlight_lowlight_solo.html
- **MAtricks:** Wings "separate the selection into the number of wings set and select devices from each wing from opposite directions". https://help.malighting.com/grandMA3/2.0/HTML/matricks_wings.html Blocks "treat blocks of fixtures as one fixture". https://help.malighting.com/grandMA3/2.0/HTML/matricks_block.html Centre-out is XWings 2 with a phase setting. https://forum.malighting.com/forum/thread/7991-circle-phaser-in-grid-layout?postID=23275
- Phase is in degrees and spreads fixtures through a loop. https://help.malighting.com/grandMA3/2.1/HTML/phaser.html
- **Layouts:** select fixtures and Assign them to a layout. Their selection-grid positions are used. Output stations placed in a layout show their network state in an indicator bar. https://help.malighting.com/grandMA3/2.3/HTML/layout_create.html

### 2.7 ChamSys MagicQ and Obsidian Onyx (lighting)

- ChamSys **FX Spread** runs from 0% (all fixtures do the same thing) to 100% (spread evenly round the cycle). Directions: Forward, Backward, Centre In, Centre Out, Rnd, Odd/Even, Left/Right, Segments, and inverted versions. Group spread options: Group, All chans, Within groups, Grid H, Grid V. https://docs.chamsys.co.uk/magicq//1.9.9.x/manual/FX_engine.html MagicQ auto-creates an "All" group per fixture type. Holding ODD/EVEN opens 3s/4s/5s. https://docs.chamsys.co.uk/magicq/manual/selecting.html
- Onyx **Grouping Tools** modes: Every, Block, Divide, Mirror, Group. A **Fan/FX** toggle decides whether offsets run across the whole selection or within the subset. https://support.obsidiancontrol.com/Content/Onyx_Manual/Programming/Fixtures_and_Groups/Groups/Using_the_Grouping_Tools/Using_the_Grouping_Tools.htm The 2D Plan can place fixtures in a line or a grid with a column count, and can **mirror placement** horizontally or vertically. https://support.obsidiancontrol.com/Content/Onyx_Manual/Programming/2D_Plan_View/Adding_Elements.htm Selection colours: grey (in programmer, not selected), bright green (selected), red (last selected), dark green (masked out). https://support.obsidiancontrol.com/Content/Onyx_Manual/Programming/Fixtures_and_Groups/Selecting_Fixtures.htm

### 2.8 Lightkey (lighting, Mac)

- Building the 2D Preview is **Step 4 of project setup**. You "drag the icons around so they approximately match the physical locations", using a top or front view. "Highlight Mode" (Cmd-Shift-H) lights the real fixture. https://lightkeyapp.com/media/pages/help/manual/0def2c007d-1793179423/Lightkey%20User%20Guide.pdf
- Effect fixture order can be "by position in Preview, short name, address, randomly, or in a custom order", including "from/to center". In a custom order, "If you assign the same number to multiple fixtures, they will behave like a single pixel. If you skip numbers you create a 'gap'." https://lightkeyapp.com/en/specs and the user guide above.

### 2.11 Safety presentation (laser rules that shape UI)

- **FDA:** audience scanning is "any scanning, projection, or reflection of laser... into audience or other accessible, uncontrolled areas". It needs a scanning safeguard so audience levels stay below Class I. https://www.fda.gov/media/72658/download Variances require that operators "be located where all propagating beam paths... and the audience can be directly observed at all times" and "immediately terminate" emission. Above Class I is not allowed below 3.0 m, or within 2.5 m laterally, of where the public stands. https://downloads.regulations.gov/FDA-2026-V-1735-0003/attachment_2.pdf Audience-scanning variances require monitoring of scan velocity, pattern size, beam power and "the beam position to determine if it is below the 3 meter horizon". "The system shall terminate all laser emissions" if any check fails. https://downloads.regulations.gov/FDA-2024-V-2154-0003/attachment_1.pdf
- **ILDA:** "All audience scanning shows must be measured", rather than "adjusted until 'it looks good'". https://www.ilda.com/resources/Safety/audience-scanning_overview_latest.pdf A software "beam attenuation map" reduces power where the beam crosses the audience. https://ilda.com/audiencescanningsafety.htm
- **PASS** fails closed. Without good power it "will crowbar... the color signal inputs, and forcibly close the shutter... [and] open the projector interlock". https://wiki.pangolin.com/doku.php?id=safety:pass It has a horizon output (pin 11) meant to drive an LED that shows whether the beam is in the audience area. https://www.pangolinlegacy.com/PASS/PASSmanual.pdf

## 3. Patterns worth stealing

1. **Two-way identify.** Source: Q-SYS ID, Dante Identify, d&b Wink, Elgato, Hue blink. Pain points: P4. Idea: clicking Identify on a card makes the laser draw a large outline digit (its rig position) and its short name. At the same time the card's border pulses. If the projector or IDN reports a hardware button press or a new announce, pulse that card too. Stop after 30 s or on a second click.

2. **Number everything at once.** Source: Resolume Identify Displays, BEYOND numbered test patterns, MadMapper "Enable Name". P4, P6. Idea: a toolbar button "Number all". Every laser draws its order number in its card's colour, so you can read the whole room in one look. The strip shows the same colours. A wrong number on stage means you drag that card.

3. **Placement that answers back.** Source: Hue lights recolour as you drag them, Lightkey's preview step, Onyx 2D plan. P4, P5. Idea: on the future 2D stage map, dragging a card left of centre makes that laser draw a solid colour for its current group (Left blue, Right red, Center white). Group membership updates as you drop. There is no separate "make groups" step.

4. **Guess, then confirm with a walk.** Source: Nanoleaf LayoutDetect, BEYOND auto-numbering, d&b array verification. P4. Idea: on discovery, guess the order from names and IPs ("L1", "Left", ".101"). Show a banner: "Order guessed from names — Confirm (walk)". The walk lights one laser at a time ("Is this one 1? ← →"). You use arrow keys to move it, then Enter. Done in under a minute for 8 lasers.

5. **Offline placeholders and a matcher.** Source: L-Acoustics Unit Matcher, d&b R1 split view, Shure WWB white/grey/green, Resolume virtual screens. P4. Idea: the rig file holds placeholder lasers. At a new venue, a two-column "Rig ↔ Found" screen lists the rig file's slots on the left and the IDN devices on the network on the right. You drag to bind. Each binding is remembered by unit id. Unbound slots stay white, bound but offline slots are grey, and live ones are green.

6. **A fixed status vocabulary with "optional".** Source: Q-SYS (OK, Compromised, Missing, Fault, Not Present), disguise head colours, Dante name colours. P6, P4. Idea: every card shows one status word from a fixed list: LIVE, ARMED-OFF, OVERLOADED, MISSING, NOT HERE (optional), FAULT. An "optional" tick on a laser turns a missing one from a red MISSING into a grey NOT HERE, so a planned 6-laser rig with one absent unit doesn't look broken.

7. **Unresolved references, fail closed.** Source: BEYOND's blocking of unknown zone names and its Unresolved tab, d&b red mismatch with a description. P5, P6, P8. Idea: when a show cue targets a group the current rig lacks (for example "Back row"), output for it is blocked. A yellow strip says "4 cues target 'Back row' — not in this rig → Map to [Center ▾] / Ignore". The mapping is saved as an alias in the rig file.

8. **Keep content and output apart.** Source: disguise Stage vs Feed, Millumin canvas mapping, Resolume slices, BEYOND zone names. P5. Idea: cues only ever name roles (zones A–D, or "Left/Right"). The zone map is the "feed", and it can be switched live. The cue editor should show nothing about projectors, just "plays on: A". This avoids per-cue wiring.

9. **A small, drawn spread vocabulary.** Source: MAtricks wings and blocks, ChamSys spread and directions, Onyx Every/Block/Divide/Mirror. P5, P3, P7. Idea: one "Spread" picker on the zone map with five icon buttons: Unison, Left→Right, Centre-out, Mirror, Odd/Even. Add one "Phase" fader from 0 to 100% (ChamSys's 0% = all the same). Leave out blocks, wings and groups as separate words.

10. **Selection order you can see.** Source: grandMA3 Highlight + Next, Lightkey custom order numbers (same number = one pixel), Onyx red for last selected. P4, P5. Idea: small order badges on each card or map dot. Typing the same badge number on two lasers makes them act as one step in chases. A "show order" button runs a slow chase so you can watch it on stage.

11. **Temporary solo that is never stored.** Source: grandMA3 Highlight/Lowlight/Solo. P4, P8. Idea: hold S over a card to have that laser draw its test pattern while the others dim to 10%. Releasing restores everything, and nothing is written to the show. While held, a banner reads "SOLO (not saved)".

12. **Template rigs with house-left naming.** Source: Pangolin's 40-laser file (L1…L4 R4…R1, house left, outside-in numbering), Sonos preset room names. P4, P1. Idea: a "Start from template" picker (2 sides, 4 line, 6 line, 4 + 2 high). Each template sets names, the order, auto-groups and one sample zone map. State "Left = house left (audience view: right)" on the screen.

13. **Output off at launch, with deliberate arming.** Source: BEYOND Enable Laser Output, LaserOS NO LASER/LASER OFF/LASER ON, PASS fail-closed. P8, P6. Idea: a top bar with a single state word: NO LASERS / SAFE (off) / LIVE. Output is always SAFE at launch. Lasers tagged for audience scanning need a second hold-to-arm (2 s press). Their cards show hatching until armed.

14. **Green keep-within, red keep-out, red wins.** Source: LaserOS safety zones. P8, P6. Idea: use the same colours for the scan limit (green) and the blocked boxes (red). Add hatching over red boxes and any audience-tagged area, so the meaning survives colour-blindness. "Show outline on laser" stops after 5 s without input, like LaserOS.

15. **Horizon indicator.** Source: PASS horizon LED, FDA "below the 3 meter horizon". P8, P6. Idea: each card's thumbnail draws a horizon line. A small "BELOW" light turns on whenever the real output has points under it, so audience exposure is visible at a glance, even from 2 m.

16. **Disabled controls give a reason.** Source: Govee hover reason, d&b description bar. P6. Idea: every greyed control has a one-line reason on hover and in the status bar: "Laser 3 MISSING", "Zone C not used by current map", "Locked during show".

17. **Re-check when something moves.** Source: Sonos ("re-run Trueplay after moving"), L-Acoustics load checker. P8, P4. Idea: when the venue profile changes, or a laser binds to a different slot, its safety zones show "UNVERIFIED AT THIS VENUE" (amber). They need a "Checked" click after the outline is projected. Output for an unverified laser is capped at a low power.

18. **Per-output latency with "negative delays others".** Source: disguise head latency (−30…+30 frames), Millumin and MadMapper per-output delay. P4. Idea: a "Latency (ms)" field on the card inspector. A negative value is explained as "delays all others by X ms". The "Number all" pattern also flashes in sync so you can match lasers by eye.

19. **Numpad as a live zone map, 0 = default.** Source: BEYOND zone hot keys. P1, P5. Idea: map the zone-map selector to a 3×3 pad (or 3 buttons). Pressing 0 means "use each cue's own target". Audience-tagged lasers never receive rerouted cues unless the cue itself is marked audience-safe (see section 4).

## 4. Anti-patterns and what users complain about

- **Live rerouting can put hot beams into the audience.** A BEYOND user's live zone selection could send hot beams to the atmospheric (audience) zone: "will cause the audience enjoy some beams in their eyes". The developer replied: "do not give ability to screw up... Realtime selection is always a... sort of dangerous." https://forums.pangolin.com/threads/zones-and-safety.2398
- **Ticking every cue by hand.** A QuickShow user with two controllers had to "right click on the cue (for each and every cue)" to target both. https://forums.pangolin.com/threads/zones-and-scanners.1500
- **Nagging, sticky warnings for missing hardware.** BEYOND pops up warnings when content targets a zone with no connected controller. A user asked for a "/dev/nul" projector. After fixing the zones, the "Check Zones" status kept flashing. https://forums.pangolin.com/threads/projector-disconnected-warnings.2062
- **Order matters more than names.** Pangolin's own template warns not to delete or reorder zones, because the MIDI map depends on numerical order. https://wiki.pangolin.com/doku.php?id=beyond:beyondperformerconsole
- **Outputs swap after a reboot.** Displays reorder if devices power up in a different order, or if EDID serials are lost. Users fall back to labelling cables. https://community.troikatronix.com/topic/8887/keeping-projector-order-some-tested-results/6?lang=en-US Resolume's own docs joke about random Windows numbering. https://resolume.com/support/en/output-setup Lesson: bind by unit id, never by list order.
- **Layout-based grouping that is fragile.** In grandMA3, "if they don't share the exact same row value it will throw off the selection order". https://reddit.com/r/GrandMA3/comments/1bp3fvy/group_from_layout_selection_is_inconsistent Users struggle to find the right phaser Width once Wings change. https://forum.malighting.com/forum/thread/8919-how-to-set-the-right-width-for-a-chase-phaser (P7)
- **Maps whose effect you can't predict.** Before Hue 4.0, "users could not understand how exactly the placement of the lights... affects the later synchronisation". https://hueblog.com/2021/05/06/philips-hue-4-0-improves-placement-of-lamps-in-the-entertainment-area One user says a light placed "behind couch... will rarely change color", so they ignore the real positions. https://reddit.com/r/Hue/comments/wjakob/gradient_light_strip_install_advice
- **Hidden assumptions about direction.** Govee assumes the strip runs clockwise from bottom-left. If it goes the other way, "the color zones will be reversed". https://www.trunetto.com/troubleshooting/smart-lighting/govee/govee-dreamview-setup Nanoleaf gestures "feel 'off'" if the layout orientation is wrong. https://support.nanoleaf.me/hc/en-us/articles/36390836295316-In-the-App-Feature-Setup-Touch-Gestures
- **Mappings you can't switch live.** In Resolume, "it is not possible... to switch advanced output maps live." https://www.facebook.com/groups/resolumegroup/posts/10160009431703277
- **Duplicate names.** In Q-SYS, two peripherals with the same name mean you "will not be able to identify which one is at fault". https://support.qsys.com/faq-%7C-can-i-have-multiple-q-sys-peripherals-share-the-same-hardware-name
- **A status button that says "on" when nothing comes out.** The LaserOS phone button "turns blue even when the cube isn't armed". https://wiki.laseros.com/docs/guides/ultra-mk2-pro-manual/ (P6)

## 5. Open questions for the app

1. **What can IDN identify by itself?** Is there any hardware identify (LED, display) over IDN, or must Identify always be a projected pattern? If it is projected, where may it draw? Inside the scan limit, above the horizon, at low power, and never on audience-tagged lasers unless they are armed? (not verified)
2. **What is the stable key for a laser across venues:** IDN unit id, MAC, IP, or name? Pattern 5 depends on this.
3. **Left/right convention:** house left (Pangolin's choice) or performer left? Should numbering run left-to-right or outside-in (Pangolin)? This affects automatic groups and the meaning of Odd/Even.
4. **Missing lasers at a gig:** should their share of a zone fold onto neighbours (Left half becomes all of Left), or simply go dark? Should "optional" lasers behave differently?
5. **Safety zones belong to a laser at a venue.** Should a venue change always mark zones "unverified" and cap power until they are checked (Pattern 17)? Or is that too much friction for a regular gig?
6. **Audience-scanning arm:** where does it live, how does show lock interact with it, and can live rerouting ever reach an audience-tagged laser?
7. **Is the strip enough, or is a 2D map needed?** Rows (front/back, high/low) suggest a map. Hue and Lightkey show that a map is fast only if dragging gives instant feedback on the real lasers.
8. **How much spread vocabulary to expose?** Five icons and one phase fader (Pattern 9) versus MAtricks-style controls, given P7.
9. **Latency units:** milliseconds or frames? Should a negative value mean "delay the others" (disguise)?
10. **Grouping in two places (P4):** if groups come from map position, should custom groups still be ticked in a separate Zone Groups tab? Or should a group simply be a lasso on the map, so there is only one place?
